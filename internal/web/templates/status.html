How to play
Enter or ZStart, choose a move, and move the text along
Arrow keysMove around the menu
MoveWhat it does
Vine LashPlain damage. Against a Lv 42, barely a scratch.
Sleep SporePuts the foe to sleep for a few turns.
Toxic ThornsPoison that hurts more every turn.
Stun PollenParalysis: the foe slows down and sometimes can't move.
Sap SiphonDrains the foe every turn and heals you. Stacks with the others.
Dew DropHeals 25 HP. You have three.

The foe can only carry one of sleep, poison or paralysis at a time, and it sometimes shakes poison or paralysis off. No grinding. Just strategy.
