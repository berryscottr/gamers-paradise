Half-Pipe Hero needs JavaScript and a canvas.
How to play
EnterStart, or play again
↓ or SpacePump at the bottom of the pipe to build speed
Z + arrowFlip tricks in the air
X + arrow (hold)Grabs. Hold longer for more points, but let go before you land
A or D (hold)Spin. Every 180 counts, but finish it before you land
PPause

Each air's tricks multiply together. Land clean to bank them and build your streak (up to ×5). Bail and the streak resets. You get 90 seconds.
